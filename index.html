Good questions. Straight answers.

START CLEAR.
GROW WITH
CONTROL.

Everything starts with the product you make and the units you want customers to check.

Can I set up my business directly?+

Yes. Manufacturers can create an account online, complete business identity checks, then add products and batches in their own workspace.

Does every pack have the same code?+

No. CounterFix is designed for a different code on every unit in a batch, with each code applied to the pack it belongs to.

What does a customer see after scanning?+

They can access the product-information experience for that pack, including the details you make available for the product and batch.

Is this a replacement for product certification?+

No. CounterFix helps connect a pack to its recorded product and batch information. It complements, and does not replace, required registrations or certifications.
