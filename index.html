Why the code on each pack matters

ONE CODE

A shared QR code can show a product page, but it cannot tell one pack from another. CounterFix Dual Defender links the code on an individual pack to the product and batch you registered.

  • Each code stays connected to the product and batch your team recorded
  • The customer sees a clear product-and-batch check for the pack they hold

Plain answers for production, quality, brand and business teams.

Is CounterFix just a QR-code generator?+

No. The QR is what the customer scans. Dual Defender is the CounterFix system working behind it, linking the pack check to the product, batch and individual unit record.

Can the codes fit our existing printing process?+

Yes. Generated codes can be incorporated into production-line printing or other established printing methods. The exact setup is matched to the manufacturer’s process.

Does every pack get a different code?+

Yes. CounterFix is designed to generate a distinct code for every unit in a batch, with each code applied to the pack it belongs to.

What does the customer see?+

They reach the product-information check for the code on their pack, including the product and batch details the manufacturer chose to make available.

Can we start with one product and one batch?+

Yes. Set up one product and the next batch you want to make checkable, then add more products and batches when you are ready.

Does this prove authenticity or replace certification?+

No. CounterFix connects a pack to its recorded product and batch information. It does not guarantee authenticity, eliminate counterfeiting or replace required registrations and certifications.
